# Changelog

## [Unreleased]
- The fab, and computers you have to build. Wafers go round a Create line once per mask layer (expose through the mask a deployer holds, develop, etch in hydrofluoric acid, dope or metallise, regrow oxide in steam, recoat resist) to make microcontrollers (10 µm PMOS), memory and microprocessors (1 µm) and advanced processors (90 nm, copper, polished each layer); a saw dices them with variable yield, and each die is bonded and sealed. Masks are chromium on glass or fused quartz, etched in ceric ammonium nitrate, and the 90 nm reticle takes a computer to write. With CC:Tweaked installed its computers, monitors, modems, drives, printers, speakers and disks are built from these parts, and a placed computer runs only beside a Power Supply on a live grid, warms the air it runs in and cuts out past 85 °C.
- Computers: with CC:Tweaked installed, thermometers, barometers, mixer-settlers, magnetomigration cells, inert drums, bloomeries and every TFMG electrical block (solar panel included) are peripherals a computer can read. A book entry lists the calls.
- Recoveries follow the plant. Crushing wheels liberate and do not multiply: a ground ore gives one dust, not one and a quarter, and the loss sits in the washer. Monazite and zircon wash at 85 in 100, chromite and the ground pegmatite minerals at five in eight, cassiterite at seven in ten; bastnäsite floats at two thirds; wolframite and scheelite wash to a concentrate three times in four before they are opened; molybdenite roasts to three fifths of its trioxide, as byproduct molybdenite floats in a copper mill.
- Nickel matte leaves the bloomery: two roasted pentlandite and a sand, superheated, smelt to two matte and a slag, as blister copper does.
- Native sulfur in the Nether: a fumarole crust on the basalt deltas and in the soul sand valleys, which grinds to sulfur dust for The Factory Must Grow's acid.
- Sulfur from oil, pyrite and spent acid. Heavy oil hydrotreated with hydrogen over a cobalt-molybdenum catalyst gives diesel and hydrogen sulfide, which a Claus unit burns to sulfur dust; pyrite lodes (massive sulfide lenses with a little chalcopyrite, sphalerite and galena) roast in a heated basin to sulfur dioxide and a hematite cinder; LPG alkylated over sulfuric acid gives gasoline and spent acid, which cracks superheated back to sulfur dioxide. The book follows the acid to where it is made.
- Sulfide ores roast only where their sulfur dioxide is caught: sphalerite, chalcopyrite, pentlandite and galena no longer roast on a campfire or in a smoker, only in a heated basin with air.
- Zinc comes with everything else in the ore. Create's zinc ore no longer generates and raw or crushed zinc no longer smelts or washes to metal; sphalerite roasts only in a heated basin with air, giving sulfur dioxide and, one roast in twenty, calomel from the Boliden-Norzink gas scrub, which limesand turns to mercury. Zinc-dust purification cements copper (a copper cement the blast furnace fire-refines to copper) and cobalt (a cobalt cake leached to cobalt sulfate) before cadmium; electrowinning plates manganese anode mud, leached with coke to manganese sulfate; the zinc retort distils cadmium first. The leach residue's ferrite dissolves in hot acid with ammonia, the jarosite process, giving more zinc liquor, a lead-silver residue the bloomery smelts to lead bullion, and jarosite, which packs into a jarosite pond.
- Create's goggles take plain glass again, as in Create; didymium glass no longer gates the first tool a factory needs.
- Heavy chemicals. Sulfuric acid by the contact process: sulfur burnt, or sulfides roasted in a heated basin, to sulfur dioxide; over vanadium pentoxide (salt-roasted from magnetite) to the trioxide; absorbed to oleum and let down to The Factory's sulfuric acid. Portland cement: limesand and clay burnt superheated to clinker, ground with scrubber gypsum (or with slag) to The Factory's cement, whose clay-and-limesand recipe is gone and whose liquid concrete now takes cement. Fertilisers: ammonium nitrate, urea, superphosphate, ammonium phosphate and NPK, stronger than bone meal and spread by dispensers; a burning mass of ammonium nitrate detonates. Phosphoric acid leaves phosphogypsum, a dead end stacked as tailings. Chemica's vanadium now comes from our pentoxide.
- Silicon from quartz to wafer: quartz and carbon in a three-electrode arc vat give liquid silicon that casts to TFMG's silicon ingot, now metallurgical silicon (TFMG's blast furnace no longer makes it); chlorine and hydrogen take it to crude trichlorosilane, TFMG's distillation tower parts off the tetrachloride, the Siemens vat deposits polysilicon, the tetrachloride is hydrogenated back, and a Czochralski boule saws to wafers, which the N and P semiconductors are now doped from. Electrolytic zinc (leach, cementation, electrowinning) with cadmium from the cementation and germanium and indium from the leach residue (GeCl4 distillation and hydrogen reduction; P204 extraction, cementation and electrorefining). Gallium from the spent Bayer liquor on a mercury cathode. Sinks: germanium-indium alloy-junction transistors, indium tin oxide displays, InGaN LED light bulbs, cadmium yellow.
- Batteries: The Factory's accumulator is built from real cells. Lead-acid from expanded lead grids pasted with litharge and formed in sulfuric acid to lead dioxide and sponge lead plates; lithium-ion (The Factory's lithium charge) from an NMC cathode (nickel, manganese and cobalt sulfates co-precipitated and calcined with lithium carbonate) or an LFP one (iron phosphate, lithium carbonate and sugar), a synthetic graphite anode, copper and aluminium foils, a polyethylene separator and lithium hexafluorophosphate in ethylene carbonate; nickel-metal hydride from a lanthanum or cerium hydride alloy and nickel hydroxide. Litharge also from lead blown in air. TFMG's lithium charge no longer takes lithium metal.
- Iron and steel: TFMG's blast furnace taps molten pig iron, not steel, half as much iron again per ore as the bloomery. Pig iron casts to TFMG's cast iron, puddles with a raw hematite or a rusty iron ingot over a superheated burner to a puddle ball that a press or the smithing hammer shingles to three iron ingots, or blows with air and limesand in any TFMG vat, with no heat or machine, to steel (a Bessemer converter). TFMG's arc furnace still makes steel.
- New minerals: flake graphite in deepslate bands, stibnite in quartz veins, pyrochlore in the carbonatite plugs, columbite-tantalite in the spodumene pegmatites, sylvite in the evaporite beds, and native gold in quartz veins and river and beach sand (55 minerals). Graphite floats with kerosene and is purified with hydrofluoric acid or in an arc vat, and presses with creosote to The Factory's graphite electrode; Acheson graphite from coke is the same item. Stibnite roasts to antimony trioxide and reduces to antimony; antimonial lead is the lead-acid accumulator's grid. Pyrochlore and niobium oxide burn with aluminium and hematite to ferroniobium, which makes HSLA steel (two heavy plates an ingot). Columbite and euxenite digest in hydrofluoric acid with potash to potassium fluorotantalate and niobium oxide, loparite chlorinates to niobium oxide, titanium tetrachloride and rare earth liquor; sodium from a Downs cell reduces the fluorotantalate to tantalum, which goes into capacitors and the superalloy. Sylvite leaches hot to potash (`c:dusts/potassium_chloride`), which is now the lithium cell's LiCl-KCl bath. Native gold pans to nuggets or amalgamates with mercury, retorts to electrum (an amalgam basin gives off mercury vapour) and parts to gold and silver by chlorine or aqua regia; vanilla gold ore mines as raw native gold. With Chemica, its tantalum and sodium are ours and its salt electrolysis is off.
- Uranium: uraninite (pitchblende) in calcite veins with silver and cobalt and in desert sandstone roll-fronts. Ground, acid-leached with pyrolusite to uranyl sulfate, thrown down as yellowcake, hydrofluorinated to green salt and fluorinated to uranium hexafluoride, enriched in The Factory's centrifuge, converted to the dioxide and sintered to fuel pellets; the tails deconvert to depleted uranium oxide. Fluorine from a potassium bifluoride cell. Zircaloy from zirconium and tin, boron carbide in an arc vat, thoria from thorium nitrate, uranium glass. Optional Create: Nuclear integration: its rods take our pellets, Zircaloy, hafnium and boron carbide, its core a beryllium reflector, and its generic ores no longer generate. Chemica's fluorine is ours, and its aqueous HF electrolysis is off.
- Siderite, iron carbonate: clay ironstone and blackband in coal-measure beds under temperate country, sparry iron in mountain limestone. Roasted on a campfire or in a smoker, it blooms and crushes like the other iron ores; raw, the bloomery refuses it.
- Chlor-alkali: brine electrolysed over a dimensionally stable anode (titanium, ruthenium, iridium) gives chlorine, caustic soda and hydrogen; caustic soda also from soda ash and lime, and it eats aluminium pipe. Aluminium by Bayer and Hall-Héroult: caustic digests bauxite to aluminate liquor and red mud, then aluminium hydroxide and alumina, electrolysed in cryolite on graphite electrodes to TFMG's aluminium ingot, replacing TFMG's straight electrolysis of bauxite. Spark plugs insulate with alumina, with iridium or platinum tips.
- Plastics: TFMG's plastic vats need a Ziegler-Natta catalyst; PVC from ethylene and chlorine as an alternative plastic sheet; natural plastic (tank, cells, TFMG's pipes) is milky and translucent; sixteen dyed plastic blocks; plastic tanks and pipes dye in place.
