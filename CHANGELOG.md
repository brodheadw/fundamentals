# Changelog

## [Unreleased]
- Uranium: uraninite (pitchblende) in calcite veins with silver and cobalt and in desert sandstone roll-fronts. Ground, acid-leached with pyrolusite to uranyl sulfate, thrown down as yellowcake, hydrofluorinated to green salt and fluorinated to uranium hexafluoride, enriched in The Factory's centrifuge, converted to the dioxide and sintered to fuel pellets; the tails deconvert to depleted uranium oxide. Fluorine from a potassium bifluoride cell. Zircaloy from zirconium and tin, boron carbide in an arc vat, thoria from thorium nitrate, uranium glass. Optional Create: Nuclear integration: its rods take our pellets, Zircaloy, hafnium and boron carbide, its core a beryllium reflector, and its generic ores no longer generate. Chemica's fluorine is ours, and its aqueous HF electrolysis is off.
- Siderite, iron carbonate: clay ironstone and blackband in coal-measure beds under temperate country, sparry iron in mountain limestone. Roasted on a campfire or in a smoker, it blooms and crushes like the other iron ores; raw, the bloomery refuses it.
- Chlor-alkali: brine electrolysed over a dimensionally stable anode (titanium, ruthenium, iridium) gives chlorine, caustic soda and hydrogen; caustic soda also from soda ash and lime, and it eats aluminium pipe. Aluminium by Bayer and Hall-Héroult: caustic digests bauxite to aluminate liquor and red mud, then aluminium hydroxide and alumina, electrolysed in cryolite on graphite electrodes to TFMG's aluminium ingot, replacing TFMG's straight electrolysis of bauxite. Spark plugs insulate with alumina, with iridium or platinum tips.
- Plastics: TFMG's plastic vats need a Ziegler-Natta catalyst; PVC from ethylene and chlorine as an alternative plastic sheet; natural plastic (tank, cells, TFMG's pipes) is milky and translucent; sixteen dyed plastic blocks; plastic tanks and pipes dye in place.
- Now a Create add-on for NeoForge; the Fabric target is dropped.
- Ore blocks for 38 minerals, generating as graded deposits, some hosted in Create's stones.
- Seven advancements, from the first mineral dug to a rare earth magnet.
- Rare earth minerals grind in Create's millstone and crushing wheels, and monazite, loparite, xenotime and euxenite wash to mixed concentrate under an encased fan.
- Solar panel: feeds The Factory Must Grow's electrical network by day.
- Solar panel: a photovoltaic panel mounted on a steel rack, feeding The Factory Must Grow's electrical network by day.
- Spodumene, in pegmatite dykes, is the source of lithium; pentlandite of nickel; our bauxite feeds TFMG's aluminium. TFMG's lithium and nickel ores no longer generate.
- Rare earth materials as items: ground minerals, mixed concentrates, the sixteen elements as oxide, ten of them also as metal, and the NdFeB and SmCo alloys.
- Initial multi-loader, multi-version project skeleton (Fabric + NeoForge, Minecraft 1.21.1).
