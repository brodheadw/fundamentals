# Ferrous Metals & Ferroalloy Ores — Research Notes

Research for the `fundamentals` mod: real-world ore → concentrate → intermediate → metal/product chains for ferrous and ferroalloy commodities. Formulas and processing routes are web-verified. Stage names are drawn from the canonical stage vocabulary.

## Processing notes (gameplay-relevant)

**Iron.** High-grade hematite is direct-shipping ore and skips magnetic beneficiation; magnetite's magnetism makes grind-then-magnet concentration cheap and is pelletized because it oxidizes exothermically to hematite on firing. Goethite/limonite (high LOI) and siderite (CO2-rich) both need a roast/calcine to drive off water/CO2 before smelting — the siderite roast also makes the ore magnetic. Taconite/BIF sits below blast-furnace cutoff grade until it is ground fine, magnetically separated, and pelletized (~65% Fe) — the backbone of the Mesabi Range. All routes converge on carbothermic blast-furnace smelting to pig iron (then BOF steel) or gas/H2 direct reduction (DRI) feeding an EAF.

**Manganese.** A dual-fate commodity: the same oxide ore either feeds a submerged-arc furnace for carbothermic reduction to ferromanganese / silicomanganese, or a reductive acid leach to MnSO4 that is electrowon into electrolytic manganese metal (EMM) or battery-grade electrolytic MnO2 (EMD). Rhodochrosite (MnCO3) must be calcined/nodulized to expel CO2 before smelting; psilomelane is hard to grind and loads the slag with barium.

**Chromium.** Chromite is chromium's only commercial ore, and because Fe reduces alongside Cr in the submerged-arc furnace, carbothermic reduction inherently yields ferrochrome — a natural gameplay reason that "pure chromium" demands an extra aluminothermic or electrolytic refining step.

**Nickel.** Two incompatible tech trees. Sulfide pentlandite goes flotation → smelt to matte → convert → refine (pressure/chloride leach + EW, or Mond carbonyl) to Class 1 metal, carrying Cu, Co, and PGMs as high-value byproducts. Laterites can't be floated (Ni is lattice-bound): iron-rich limonite takes the HPAL hydromet route to MHP (and significant byproduct cobalt), while magnesia-rich saprolite/garnierite takes the energy-intensive pyromet RKEF route to ferronickel / nickel pig iron.

**Cobalt.** Roughly 98% of cobalt is a byproduct — of copper in the DRC Copperbelt (carrollite/heterogenite, recovered from the Cu leach raffinate by SX + precipitation) and of nickel (pentlandite and limonite laterite). Cobaltite (CoAsS) and erythrite (Co3(AsO4)2·8H2O) are minor/historical; the arsenic is a smelting hazard and erythrite's pink "cobalt bloom" is a prospecting indicator. Gate Co behind Cu or Ni production rather than as a standalone mine.

**Molybdenum.** Chiefly a byproduct of porphyry-copper flotation; after selective flotation the concentrate is oxidatively roasted to MoO3 technical oxide (the roaster is the world's only economic rhenium source), then either H2-reduced to Mo powder or metallothermically reduced to ferromolybdenum.

**Tungsten.** Wolframite (dense, weakly magnetic) separates by gravity + magnetic sep from associated cassiterite (tin); scheelite (non-magnetic Ca-tungstate) routes through froth flotation. Both converge on the shared APT (ammonium paratungstate) → WO3 intermediate, finished by H2 reduction to W powder or EAF smelting to ferrotungsten.

**Vanadium.** The dominant primary source, titanomagnetite, cross-links three metals: iron is the main product, vanadium reports to the steelmaking slag (salt roast → leach → AMV → V2O5 → aluminothermic ferrovanadium), and titanium reports to the smelting slag. Vanadinite couples V with lead; carnotite couples V with uranium (yellowcake), linking the vanadium chain to the nuclear chain.

**Titanium.** Ilmenite, rutile, and leucoxene are co-separated from heavy-mineral sands by the gravity → magnetic → electrostatic flowsheet (alongside zircon and REE-bearing monazite). ~90% of titanium ends as TiO2 pigment, not metal. High-grade feed (natural rutile, TiO2 slag, synthetic rutile, leucoxene) enters the chloride route: carbochlorination to TiCl4, then Kroll magnesium reduction to titanium sponge, with the MgCl2 byproduct electrolyzed back to Mg + Cl2. Note: the Kroll step is magnesiothermic (metallothermic) reduction, which is not in the canonical stage vocabulary — it is modeled below under `Refining` (chlorination/distillation) plus `AluminothermicReduction` as a metallothermic proxy. Consider adding a dedicated `MetallothermicReduction` stage.
