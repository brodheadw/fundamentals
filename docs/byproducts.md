## Tailings

Waste that has no use is packed into a block and put back in the ground, as a tailings dam or a dry stack holds it: Clarifier Tailings (`fundamentals:clarifier_sludge_block`), Block of Monazite Residue (`fundamentals:monazite_residue_block`), Bauxite Residue (`fundamentals:red_mud_block`). Anything else can be burnt in lava or thrown in the void, as in the vanilla game.
